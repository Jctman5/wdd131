Undertow Band – Site Plan

Site Name

Undertow Official

This name clearly represents the band and keeps branding consistent across platforms, making the website easy for fans and promoters to find.

Site Purpose

The Undertow website will serve as the central hub for show dates, music releases, images, band updates, and a booking contact form.

Scenarios

  • What are the upcoming Undertow show dates and locations?
  • Where can I listen to the newest Undertow releases?
  • How can I contact the band for booking or promotions?

Color Scheme

Electric Blue (#1e90ff) – Headings and accents

Charcoal Black (#0d0d0d) – Background

Light Gray (#e6e6e6) – Body text

Typography

Headings: Oswald or sans-serif

Body: Roboto or sans-serif

Logo: Custom stylized font
